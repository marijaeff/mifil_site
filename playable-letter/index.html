GOOD TO KNOW

Little questions.

Is this a physical gift or a digital one?

It's a personalized browser-based game. You can share its link digitally or use a printed QR code as a physical surprise.

Do they need to install an app?

No. The adventure is designed to open in a compatible browser on a phone.

What can I personalize?

The favorite items, questions, box clues, secret word, final letter and visual style are the main elements. Specific requests are confirmed when planning the gift.

Does the recipient get to keep the letter?

Yes, the personal letter can be downloaded as a PDF after the adventure.

How much does it cost, and how long will it take?

The price and delivery timeline will be confirmed before purchase.
